What is this study about?

AI assistants ("agents") can read e-mails, documents and web pages and then act on your behalf, for example by sending messages or payments. We are studying how the interface of such an assistant can help people decide which actions should be allowed.

What will I do?

You will watch an AI assistant work on short everyday tasks in a simulated e-mail, calendar, cloud-drive, travel and banking environment: two practice tasks, then 16 tasks. Sometimes the assistant will ask for your approval before it acts. Some tasks may include problems that you should catch. Afterwards you will answer a short questionnaire about your experience. The study takes about minutes.

Are there risks?

No real accounts, money or messages are involved. Everything happens in a simulation. You may find some tasks tedious. You can stop at any time.

What data are collected?

  • Your decisions in the interface and how long they took.
  • Your questionnaire answers.
  • Basic demographics (age range, gender, programming experience).

We do not collect your name, e-mail address or IP address in the study application. Data are linked only to a participant code and are stored on secure institutional storage for years. Anonymised data may be shared with other researchers for replication.
